**Table S3** (`Source_data/Table_S3.csv`)

Table S3. Differentially abundant lipids associated with MASLD histological features. This table summarizes lipids significantly associated with steatosis, inflammation and fibrosis in liver tissue, adjusted for age, sex, and BMI. For each lipid, the table reports the log2 fold change (logFC), average expression (AveExpr), t-statistic (t), nominal P-value (P.Value), and false discovery rate-adjusted P-value (adj.P.Val, FDR). The “Direction” column indicates whether the lipid is upregulated (Up) or downregulated (Down) in the respective condition. Lipids are grouped by phenotype, allowing identification of shared and exclusive lipidomic alterations across the steatosis, inflammation and fibrosis histological features of MASLD.
