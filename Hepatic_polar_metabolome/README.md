**Table S4** (`Source_data/Table_S4.csv`)

Table S4. Differentially abundant metabolites associated with MASLD histological features. This table summarizes metabolites significantly associated with steatosis and inflammation in liver tissue, adjusted for age, sex, and BMI. For each metabolite, the table reports the log2 fold change (logFC), average expression (AveExpr), t-statistic (t), nominal P-value (P.Value), and false discovery rate-adjusted P-value (adj.P.Val, FDR). The “Direction” column indicates whether the metabolite is upregulated (Up) or downregulated (Down) in the respective condition, m/z value and retention time. Metabolites are grouped by phenotype, allowing identification of shared and exclusive metabolic alterations across the steatosis and inflammation histological features of MASLD. No metabolites reached statistical significance in fibrosis at FDR ≤ 0.1.
